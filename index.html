How to open your album

  1. 01
    Keep your album details ready

    Use the mobile number and unique Album Code shared with you by SARITA PHOTO.

  2. 02
    Open the private E-Album

    Tap the button below. The secure album page will open in a new page.

  3. 03
    Enter your details

    Type your mobile number and Album Code carefully, then continue to view your album.

  4. 04
    View and enjoy your photos

    Move between photos using the next/previous controls. On a phone, you can also swipe where supported. Use the available share or save options if shown.
